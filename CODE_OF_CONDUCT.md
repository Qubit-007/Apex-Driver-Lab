# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Apex Driver Lab welcomes respectful participation from people with varied backgrounds and experience levels. Project spaces should remain professional, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in discussion and review.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal information without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
